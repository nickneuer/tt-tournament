1. Number of wins: The primary factor for ranking is the total number of matches won. The player with the most wins places highest.

2. Head-to-head record: If two or more players have the same number of wins, their positions are decided by their respective win-loss record within the subset of matches played only between the tied players.

3. Games percentage: If players are still tied after comparing their head-to-head records, their rank is decided by the percentage of games won versus games lost, but only for the matches involving the tied players.

4. Points percentage: If a tie persists after calculating the games percentage, the total points won versus points lost is used as the deciding factor, again, only for the matches involving the tied players.

5. Random draw: In the rare event that all the above steps fail to break the tie, the final ranking is decided by a random draw.
